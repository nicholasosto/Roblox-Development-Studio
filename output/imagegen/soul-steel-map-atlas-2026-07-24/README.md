## Continuity notes

- The current five source-canonical domains are Blood, Decay, Spirit, Robot, and Fateless. The Void Plane is canon cosmology; the brain-native `Void Domain` remains speculative and is not drawn as a sixth spoke.
- Plate 03 treats the brighter cracks as shared Sundering/Veil damage rather than a Void-domain road. Their exact density and placement are speculative.
- The north landing pad and bus-style civic shuttle are new proposed infrastructure. They occupy the sixth face of the hexagon but do not create a sixth gate or modify the navigation graph.
- Plate 03 deliberately uses one open interior plane. Its six readable sections are material and floor-band changes, not separate rooms, rings, or courtyards.
- The approved topology establishes adjacency but not true cardinal geography. Current Crossroads material also contains directional ambiguity, including both Blood and Spirit described toward the east in one zone spec. These plates therefore avoid claiming a true north.
- `Steel City` canon places Forge Citadel inside the city and the Automated Mines beneath the Robot domain. The three-zone chain on Plate 04 is progression, not a claim that the three sites form a literal straight surface road.
- Newly generated spatial arrangements remain speculative until an owner ratifies them. The images deliberately label those areas as interpretive, proposed, or planning overlays.
