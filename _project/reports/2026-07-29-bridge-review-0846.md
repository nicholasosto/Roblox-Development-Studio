## Surprises

- **The ADR citation was the disguise, not the defense.** The rojo case was catchable because it cited
  nothing. Its worse sibling — "Per decision 0015 there is deliberately no `spec` entity kind" — reads
  as ratified and survives exactly the check a careful reader would run. The habit "check whether it
  cites an ADR" is weaker than assumed; the durable check is "does the cited ADR actually say it."
- **Correcting a claim does not correct its copies.** CLAUDE.md's rojo table was fixed on 07-28; the
  agent built to verify such claims still carried the pre-convergence baseline a day later. Doc repairs
  need a grep for downstream copies, and checkers are the copies that matter most.
- **The recurrence lens needs a design filter.** "Uncommitted at close" recurs in 5 of 13 engrams — more
  than any real friction theme — and is the commit ritual working correctly. Frequency alone would have
  proposed a routine to fight an intentional practice.
- **Follow-through was better than expected.** Every `→ memory` candidate with a named home was actually
  written. The drain's value in this window was the *unmarked* recurrence (pass 5b), not the marked queue.

## Decisions made

- **D1**: The window's `→ landed in CLAUDE.md` route is treated as a defect pattern, not a valid
  placement — rationale claims route to `/new decision`, facts may be written directly. Recorded in
  memory rather than as an ADR, since it governs how *this agent* works, not how the project is built.
- **D2**: Three separately-marked routine candidates consolidate into one `doc-vs-disk-sweep` (P8)
  dispatching the existing `disk-witness`, rather than three schedules. One instrument already owns
  every probe; three routines would triple the cadence noise for one signal.
- **D3**: Three marked `→ routine` lines are rejected rather than deferred (P9), with reasons recorded,
  because a deferred routine returns to every future review and a rejected one does not.
